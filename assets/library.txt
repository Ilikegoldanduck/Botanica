The Scorch Part 1
*No one notices a spark. Everyone sees the fire after*
The Scorch. A devestating conflict between demon and man. The lives lost numbered in the tens of thousands.
The conflict started after adventurer deaths started increasing dramatically in the caverns of Vulcana. Sending a scouting party ahead, Aquafall, the church in the region representing Flow, found there to be nothing in the caves. They believed there to be simply more powerful monsters in the caverns. However, the cave became feared due to the deaths, leading to no sane adventurer entering the caves. However, the scouting party did infact miss something, the crack in reality.
The crack only got worse and worse as years passed. Demons slowly poured out of the dimensional rift and into Terra. By the time Aquafall realized, it was too late. Demons poured out of the caves in one giant push, burning the forest and killing locals within the first hour of the break.
Retaliation was necessary for survival. The Emerald Church oversaw the immediate mobilization of adventurer parties, no matter how large or small. These parties were to fight and push against the demons, sending them back to the Under, what the church called it to be. The church also created refugee camps for displaced citizens in the area.

Flow
*For those who believe in plasticity and adaptability*
One of the major religions in Terra, Flow celebrates the deity of water. They believe that one day, they'll rain Terra Ignis away, flooding it and bringing life to what is a wasteland. The deity of water doesn't seem to have a name as the believers believe that names root you down to an identity. They refer to the deity as "God" or "Deity of Water".
Most Flow Churches reside by the water. They can be put into 3 categories: Minor churches, Major churches and the Cathedral.
Minor Churches are unnamed and reside near lakes and rivers. They are a poor man's church, providing to the commonrs that practice their religion.
Major churches are named for organization and oversee the minor churches in their region. They are found near beaches with their power and influence ranging with their distance from the Great Sea. Usually, the middle class travels to this church.
The Cathedral is the leading church of Flow. It resides in the center of the Great Sea. Outsiders and common worshippers don't know how it floats or where it goes. Only the high priests of Flow know the location at all times and guide those who seek the Cathedral out.
Unlike other major religions, FLow doesn't operate on a strict schedule of worship. This lets believers worship the god whenever they have the time. Even so, most believers try keeping it below one moth between worships. This lenient schedule makes Flow the most popular religion for commoners.

Emral
*Those who believe in Nature, she'll restore. Those who don't, she'll destroy*
In the beginning, there were two. The sky and the ground. Vin, the ground, created us. She gave us life and sustained us with water adn fire. She held up the sky that showed us time. She protected us from harm.
Vin was benevolent, healing and helping those in need. She was perfect in every sense. Someone we looked up to in need.
One day, fire rebelled. Vin, with Mist, casted him out of our lands. He wanders Ignis to this day, never to see the peace in Terra.
Vin's power, along with Mist, dwindled after she exiled fire. She resets as fire grows from afar, ready to come back. We must believe that she awakes, before fire becomes too powerful. We must protect this earth as Vin has done for us. We must protect others as Vin has done for us. We must defeat fire as Vin has done for us.

Terra Ignis
*For the curious. For the brave. For the adventurous. But not for the stupid.*
Terra Ignis is what surrounds the main region of Terra. Never, and I say this with a serious mind, never walk into Terra Ignis. Invisible flames burn the region. Fire that never dies, goes out, and kills. Ignis is marked with a bright yellow sand-like powder. It also gives off a stench resembling close to a rotting corpse.
Religions such as Flow and Emral believe that Ignis comes from the Fire Deity. This theory is plausible, considering the elemental properties that reside in the region. However, other religions belive that the Under is the domain of the Fire Deitym leading to the debate of the true ownder of Terra Ignis. I believe in neither case.
Adventures into Ignis have mostly gone unsuccessful. THe last one ended in failure. Eyewitnesses report explosions, screams and metal creaking. There there was nothing. No metal. No people. No craters.

Mana and its applications
*Concepts into Reality*
Mana. Creative energy that has been said to come from Gods. Application comes from envisioning in the mind and creating.
A person's mana stores can only be increased up to 50% more than the amount they're born with. Someone can only become a proficient spellcaster by birth.
Utilizing Mana comes from speaking in *God's Hand*. However, because of the complexity of the language, most casters resort to incantations.
Incantations come in 3 different forms: Thinking, Speaking and Writing. Thinking incantations works through speaking the language within your mind while creating an image of the spell. However, speaking helps most sorcerers with casting a multitude of spells due to relying on muscle memory rather than thoughts. Written is most commonly observed in scholarly texts.
